Articles & Content

A collection of my written work: essays, opinion pieces, and articles on education, society, and other topics I care about. This repository serves as an archive and a home for drafts, submissions, and published pieces.

About

I'm Hariharan M, currently in the pre-final year of my undergraduate degree. Writing is where I work through ideas I don't get to explore in a classroom, mostly around education, nation-building, and how systems could work better for the people inside them. This repo is where that writing lives.

Structure

Each article is kept as a standalone Markdown file, named in lowercase with hyphens. Where relevant, an article may include:

A short abstract at the top
The main body, broken into headed sections
A note on where it was submitted or published, if applicable
Purpose

This repository is primarily a personal archive: a place to track drafts as they evolve, keep finished pieces in one place, and revisit older ideas. Some entries may be submissions to writing competitions or publications; those are noted individually within each file.
